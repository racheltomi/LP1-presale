FAQ

Questions? Answered.

When does HGA reopen?

Pre-launch pricing opens November 23, 2026 and runs until January 31, 2027. The grading calendar opens for booking on January 18, 2027, and grading begins February 1, 2027.

What happens after I purchase?

Your slots are saved to your account. On January 18, 2027 we email you an invite to book your grading date, and your pre-launch credit applies automatically. Every grade is back within 30 days of your grading date.

When can I submit?

Once you've booked your grading date, ship your cards any time before that date. Your slots never expire, so you can pick the date that suits you.

Which cards qualify?

Sports cards and trading cards, including Pokémon, Yu-Gi-Oh!, Magic and more. Pre-launch slots cover standard size cards; booklets, folds and decks are booked separately. Cards valued under $10,000 have no value upcharge; above $10,000, a 2% insurance and handling fee applies.

Can I transfer my purchase?

No. Pre-launch purchases are tied to the purchasing account and can't be transferred.
